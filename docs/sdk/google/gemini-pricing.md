### Additional Notes

* **Thinking / “Thinking models”**: The 2.5 family introduces “thinking” capabilities (i.e. internal reasoning / chain-of-thought) more robustly. In many cases thinking is turned on by default (Pro, Flash), or can be enabled with a budget (Flash-Lite). This adds internal token generation for reasoning, which counts toward pricing when enabled. ([Google AI for Developers][2])
* **Caching**:
“Context caching” is supported in both 2.5 and 2.0 versions in many models. ([Google AI for Developers][2])
• Google also introduced *implicit caching* for Gemini 2.5, which helps reduce cost/overhead of repetitive context. ([Google Developers Blog][5])
* **Rate limits** differ depending on whether model is free vs paid tier, and whether stable or preview / experimental. The Pro models especially have tighter rate limits. ([Google AI for Developers][2])
